Theorem 1.8.15. Inequalities and squares.

Let \(a\) be a positive real number. We have
\begin{align*} x^2< a \amp \iff -\sqrt{a}\leq x\leq \sqrt{a}\\ \amp \iff x\in (-\sqrt{a}, \sqrt{a})\\ x^2> a \amp \iff x< -\sqrt{a} \text{ or } x> \sqrt{a} \\ \amp \iff x\in (-\infty, -\sqrt{a})\cup (\sqrt{a}, \infty)\text{.} \end{align*}
