Procedure 1.5.22. Solving polynomial equations.

Consider a polynomial equation of the form
\begin{equation} \anpoly =b\text{.}\tag{1.42} \end{equation}
To solve this equation for \(x\text{,}\) proceed as follows.
  1. Use algebra to bring all terms to the left side (using addition/subtraction), obtaining an equation of the form
    \begin{equation} a_nx^n+a_{n-1}x^{n-1}+\cdots +(a_0-b)=0\text{.}\tag{1.43} \end{equation}
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Factor the polynomial on the left side of (1.43) as much as possible to obtain an equation of the form
    \begin{equation} f_1(x)f_2(x)\cdots f_r(x)=0\text{.}\tag{1.44} \end{equation}
    🔗
    🔗
  3. The solutions of the original equation (1.42) are precisely the roots of the polynomials \(f_1,f_2,\dots, f_r\text{.}\)
