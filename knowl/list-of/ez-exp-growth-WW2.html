Find a formula for \(P = f(t)\text{,}\) the size of the population that begins in year \(t = 0\) with \(2210\) members and decreases at a \(3.8\)% annual rate. Assume that time is measured in years.
🔗
\(P = f(t) =\)
🔗
Answer.
\(2210\cdot 0.962^{t}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
The starting value is \(a=2210\text{.}\) The growth rate is \(r=-0.038\)% (note that it is negative), so \(b=1+r=0.962\text{.}\) The formula is
🔗
\begin{equation*} P = {2210\cdot 0.962^{t}} \end{equation*}
