Exercise 4.

Find an equation for the line passing through the points \((1,2)\) and \((-1,0)\text{.}\) Write your answer in the form \(y=ax+b\text{.}\)
🔗
Solution.
The equation of a nonvertical line passing through two points \((x_{1},y_{1})\) and \((x_{2},y_{2})\) is \(y-y_{1}=m(x-x_{1})\text{,}\) where the slope \(m=\dfrac{y_2-y_1}{x_2-x_1}\text{.}\) Note that it does not matter which point is first and which is second - they form the same line. For the given line, the slope is \(m=\dfrac{2-0}{1-(-1)}=1\text{,}\) so the equation of the line is \(y-2=1(x-1)\text{,}\) or \(y=x+1\text{.}\)
