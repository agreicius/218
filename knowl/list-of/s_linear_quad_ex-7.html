Exercise 7.

Find an equation for the line with \(y\)-intercept 3 and slope \(-2\text{.}\) Write your answer in the form \(ax+by+c=0\text{.}\)
🔗
Solution.
If the line has \(y\)-intercept \(3\text{,}\) it passes through the point \((0,3)\text{.}\) Hence, the equation of the line is \(y-3=-2(x-0)\text{,}\) or \(2x+y-3=0\text{.}\)
