Exercise 8.

Find an equation for the line parallel to \(2x-3y=1\text{,}\) passing through the point \((1,-1)\text{.}\) Write your answer in the form \(ax+by+c=0\text{.}\)
🔗
Solution.
Parallel lines have the same slope. To find the slope of the given line, write it in the form \(y=\dfrac{2}{3}x-\dfrac{1}{3}\text{.}\) This line has slope \(2/3\text{,}\) and hence, any line parallel to it has slope \(2/3\text{.}\) Thus, the equation of the line is \(y-(-1)=\dfrac{2}{3}(x-1)\text{,}\) or \(2x-3y-5=0\text{.}\)
