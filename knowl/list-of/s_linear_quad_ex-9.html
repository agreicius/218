Exercise 9.

Let \(l_{1}\) be the line that passes through the points \((1,2)\) and \((-1,3)\text{.}\) Find an equation for the line that is parallel to \(l_{1}\) with \(x\)-intercept 2. Write your answer in the form \(ax+by+c=0\text{.}\)
🔗
Solution.
The line is parallel to \(l_{1}\text{,}\) so it has the same slope \(m=\dfrac{-1-1}{3-2}=-2\text{,}\) and passes through the point \((2,0)\text{.}\) Hence, its equation is \(y-0=-2(x-2)\text{,}\) or \(2x+y-4=0\text{.}\)
