Exercise 1.

Let \(f(x)=\dfrac{x}{x-2}\) and \(g(x)=\dfrac{2x}{x-1}\text{.}\) Find
  1. \(\displaystyle (f-g)(-2)\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. \(\displaystyle (f+g)(1)\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. \(\displaystyle (f-g)(2)\)
    🔗
    🔗
  4. \(\displaystyle \dfrac{f}{g}(3)\)
    🔗
    🔗
  5. \(\displaystyle \dfrac{f}{g}(0)\)
    🔗
    🔗
  6. \(\displaystyle g\cdot g(3)\)
    🔗
    🔗
  7. \(\displaystyle f\circ g(1)\)
    🔗
    🔗
  8. \(f\circ g(2)=f(g(2))=f\left(\dfrac{2\cdot 2}{2-1}\right)=f(4)=\dfrac{4}{4-2}=2\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  9. \(\displaystyle g \circ f(x)\)
    🔗
    🔗
  10. \(\displaystyle f \circ f (x)\)
    🔗
    🔗
🔗
Solution.
  1. \((f-g)(-2)=f(-2)-g(-2)=\dfrac{-2}{-2-2}-\dfrac{2(-2)}{-2-1}=\dfrac{1}{2}-\dfrac{4}{3}=-\dfrac{5}{6}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. \(g(x)\) is not defined for \(x=1\text{,}\) so \((f+g)(1)\) is undefined.
    🔗
    🔗
  3. \(f(x)\) is not defined for \(x=2\text{,}\) so \((f-g)(2)\) is undefined.
    🔗
    🔗
  4. \(\dfrac{f}{g}(3)=\dfrac{f(3)}{g(3)}=\dfrac{\dfrac{3}{3-2}}{\dfrac{2\cdot 3}{3-1}}=\dfrac{3}{3}=1\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  5. \(g(0)=0\text{,}\) so \(\dfrac{f}{g}(0)\) is undefined.
    🔗
    🔗
  6. \(\displaystyle g\cdot g(3)=g(3)\cdot g(3)=3\cdot 3=9.\)
    🔗
    🔗
  7. \(f\circ g(1)=f(g(1))\text{,}\) but \(g\) is not defined at \(1\text{,}\) so \(f\circ g(1)\) is undefined.
    🔗
    🔗
  8. \(g\circ f(x)=g(f(x))=g\left(\dfrac{x}{x-2}\right)=\dfrac{2\cdot\dfrac{x}{x-2}}{\dfrac{x}{x-2}-1}=\dfrac{\dfrac{2x}{x-2}}{\dfrac{2}{x-2}}\\=\dfrac{2x}{x-2}\cdot \dfrac{2}{x-2}=x\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  9. \(f\circ f(x)=f(f(x))=f\left(\dfrac{x}{x-2}\right)=\dfrac{\dfrac{x}{x-2}}{\dfrac{x}{x-2}-2}=\dfrac{\dfrac{x}{x-2}}{\dfrac{-x+4}{x-2}}=\dfrac{x}{4-x}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
