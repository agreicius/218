Exercise 2.

Solve the following equations.
  1. \(\displaystyle x^{2}-5x-14=0\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. \(\displaystyle x^{2}=x\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. \(\displaystyle x^{2}=3x-5\)
    🔗
    🔗
  4. \(\displaystyle x^{2}-x+(x+\dfrac{1}{2})^{2}=8\dfrac{1}{4}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  5. \(\displaystyle (3x-2)-(x-3)^{2}=-11\)
    🔗
    🔗
  6. \(\displaystyle x+\dfrac{1}{x}=5,\ x\neq 0\)
    🔗
    🔗
  7. \(\displaystyle \dfrac{x+1}{x-1}=3-\dfrac{x-2}{x+2},\ x\neq1,x\neq-2\)
    🔗
    🔗
🔗
Solution.
  1. Use the quadratic formula.
    \begin{equation*} x_{1/2}=\dfrac{5\pm\sqrt{25+4\cdot 14}}{2}=\dfrac{5\pm\sqrt{81}}{2}=\dfrac{5\pm9}{2}. \end{equation*}
    Thus, \(x_{1}=\dfrac{5-9}{2}=-2\) and \(x_{2}=\dfrac{5+9}{2}=7\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. \begin{align*} x^{2}-x\amp=0\\ x(x-1)&=0\\ x=0 \text{ or }\amp x=1 \end{align*}
    🔗
  3. The equation is equivalent to \(x^{2}-3x+5=0\text{.}\) Since the discriminant is \(D=(-3)^{2}-4\cdot 5=9-20=-11<0\text{,}\) the equation has no real solutions.
    🔗
    🔗
  4. \begin{equation*} x^{2}-x+(x+\dfrac{1}{2})^{2}=8\dfrac{1}{4} \end{equation*}
    \begin{equation*} x^{2}-x+x^{2}+x+\dfrac{1}{4}=8\dfrac{1}{4} \end{equation*}
    \begin{equation*} 2x^{2}=8 \end{equation*}
    \begin{equation*} x^{2}=4 \end{equation*}
    \begin{equation*} x_{1/2}=\pm2. \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗
  5. \begin{equation*} (3x-2)-(x-3)^{2}=-11 \end{equation*}
    \begin{equation*} 3x-2-(x^{2}-6x+9)=-11 \end{equation*}
    \begin{equation*} 3x-2-x^{2}+6x-9+11=0 \end{equation*}
    \begin{equation*} -x^{2}+9x=0 \end{equation*}
    \begin{equation*} x(-x+9)=0 \end{equation*}
    \begin{equation*} x=0 \text{ or }x=9. \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗
  6. \begin{equation*} x+\dfrac{1}{x}=5 \end{equation*}
    \begin{equation*} x^{2}+1=5x \end{equation*}
    \begin{equation*} x^{2}-5x+1=0 \end{equation*}
    \begin{equation*} x_{1/2}=\dfrac{5\pm\sqrt{25-4}}{2} \end{equation*}
    \begin{equation*} x_{1/2}=\dfrac{5\pm \sqrt{21}}{2}. \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗
  7. \begin{equation*} \dfrac{x+1}{x-1}=3-\dfrac{x-2}{x+2} \end{equation*}
    \begin{equation*} (x+1)(x+2)=3(x-1)(x+2)-(x-2)(x-1) \end{equation*}
    \begin{equation*} x^{2}+x+2x+2=3(x^{2}-x+2x-2)-(x^{2}-2x-x+2) \end{equation*}
    \begin{equation*} x^{2}+3x+2=3x^{2}+3x-6-x^{2}+3x-2 \end{equation*}
    \begin{equation*} -x^{2}-3x+10=0 \end{equation*}
    \begin{equation*} x^{2}+3x-10=0 \end{equation*}
    \begin{equation*} x_{1/2}=\dfrac{-3\pm\sqrt{9+40}}{2}=\dfrac{-3\pm7}{2}. \end{equation*}
    Hence, the solutions are \(x_{1}=\dfrac{-3-7}{2}=-5\) and \(x_{2}=\dfrac{-3+7}{2}=2\text{.}\)
