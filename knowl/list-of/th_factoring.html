Theorem 1.2.31. Quadratic factoring identities.

Let \(a,b\in \R\text{.}\)
  1. Difference of squares.
    \(\displaystyle a^2-b^2=(a-b)(a+b).\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Binomial square.
    \(\displaystyle (a+b)^2=a^2+2ab+b^2.\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. Distribution.
    \(\displaystyle a^2+ab=a(a+b).\)
