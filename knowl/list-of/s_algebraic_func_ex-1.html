Exercise 1.

Find the implied domain of the following power functions.
  1. \(\displaystyle f_{1}(x)=\sqrt{2}x^{2/3}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. \(\displaystyle f_{2}(x)=-3x^{-3}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. \(\displaystyle f_{3}(x)=2x^{-5/2}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  4. \(\displaystyle f_{4}(x)=\dfrac{x^{1/4}}{4}\)
    🔗
    🔗
Then find \(f_{1}(-8), f_{2}(-3), f_{3}(4), f_{4}(16)\text{.}\)
🔗
Solution.
  1. The domain is \(\mathbb{R}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. The domain is \(\mathbb{R}\setminus \{0\}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. The domain is \((0,\infty)\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  4. The domain is \([0,\infty)\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  5. \begin{equation*} f_{1}(-8)=\sqrt{2}(-8)^{2/3}=\sqrt{2}((-2)^{3})^{2/3}=\sqrt{2}(-2)^{2}=4\sqrt{2} \end{equation*}
    🔗
    \begin{equation*} f_{2}(-3)=-3\cdot(-3)^{-3}=-3\cdot \dfrac{1}{(-3)^3}=-3\cdot \dfrac{1}{-27}=\dfrac{1}{9} \end{equation*}
    🔗
    \begin{equation*} f_{3}(4)=2\cdot 4^{-5/2}=2\cdot (2^{2})^{-5/2}=2\cdot 2^{(-5)}=2^{-4}=\dfrac{1}{16} \end{equation*}
    🔗
    \begin{equation*} f_{4}(16)=\dfrac{16^{1/4}}{4}=\dfrac{(2^4)^{1/4}}{4}=\dfrac{2}{4}=\dfrac{1}{2}. \end{equation*}
