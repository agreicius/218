Exercise 2.

Simplify the expression to one involving only positive exponents. Assume that everything is well-defined.
  1. \(\displaystyle \left(\dfrac{x^{-1/4}y}{xz^2}\right)^{-4}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. \(\displaystyle \dfrac{(x^3y)^{-1}(8^{-1}x^4y^3)}{(2x^2y^{-3})^{-2}}\)
    🔗
    🔗
🔗
Solution.
  1. \(\displaystyle \left(\dfrac{x^{-1/4}y}{xz^2}\right)^{-4}=\dfrac{(x^{-1/4})^{-4}y^{-4}}{x^{-4}(z^2)^{-4}}=\dfrac{xy^{-4}}{x^{-4}z^{-8}}=\dfrac{xx^4z^8}{y^4}=\dfrac{x^5z^8}{y^4}.\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. \(\displaystyle \dfrac{(x^3y)^{-1}(8^{-1}x^4y^3)}{(2x^2y^{-3})^{-2}}=\dfrac{x^{-3}y^{-1}8^{-1}x^4y^3}{2^{-2}x^{-4}y^6}=\dfrac{xy^28^{-1}}{2^{-2}x^{-4}y^6}=\dfrac{xx^42^2}{8y^{6-2}}=\dfrac{x^5}{2y^4}.\)
    🔗
