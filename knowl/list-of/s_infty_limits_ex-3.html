Exercise 3.

Let \(f(x)=\dfrac{x^4}{12}-\dfrac{x^2}{2}+2x\text{.}\) Find all \(x\) such that \(f''(x)>0\text{.}\)
🔗
Solution.
\begin{equation*} f'(x)=\dfrac{4x^3}{12}-\dfrac{2x}{2}+2=\dfrac{x^3}{3}-x+2 \end{equation*}
and
\begin{equation*} f''(x)=\dfrac{3x^2}{3}-1=x^{2}-1. \end{equation*}
Thus, we need to find all \(x\) such that \(x^{2}-1>0\text{.}\) Using a sign chart, we conclude that the solution is \((-\infty,-1)\cup(1,\infty)\text{.}\)
