Exercise 5.

Determine the values of \(a,b,c\) so that the polynomials \(f(x)\) and \(g(x)\) are equal.
  1. \(f(x)=2(x-3)^{2}-4(x+1)\)
    🔗
    \(g(x)=(a-1)x^{2}+(b+5)x+c.\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. \(f(x)=(x-3)(2x^{2}-4x+5)+(x+2)(x^{2}-3x+1)\)
    🔗
    \(g(x)=(a+b)x^{3}+(2a-c)x^{2}+(b+3c)x+11.\)
    🔗
    🔗
🔗
Solution.
  1. Two polynomials are equal if the corresponding coefficients are equal. We have \(f(x)=2x^{2}-12x+18-4x-4=2x^{2}-16x+14\text{,}\) so we get \(a-1=2, b+5=-16, c=14\text{,}\) i.e. \(a=3,b=-21,c=14\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. We have \(f(x)=2x^{3}-4x^{2}+5x-6x^{2}+12x-15+x^{3}-3x^{2}+x+2x^{2}-6x+2=3x^{3}-11x^{2}+12x-13\text{.}\) Thus \(a+b=3,2a-c=-11,b+3c=12\text{,}\) i.e., \(a=-\dfrac{24}{5},b=\dfrac{39}{5},c=\dfrac{7}{5}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
