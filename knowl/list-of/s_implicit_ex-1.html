Exercise 1.

Assuming \(y=y(x)\) is a differentiable function, find \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}\) if \(x^{2}y+xy^{2}=x-1\text{.}\)
🔗
Solution.
\begin{equation*} x^{2}y(x)+x(y(x))^{2}=x-1 \end{equation*}
\begin{equation*} \dfrac{d}{dx}[x^{2}y(x)+x(y(x))^{2}]=\dfrac{d}{dx}[x-1] \end{equation*}
\begin{equation*} 2xy(x)+x^{2}y'(x)+(y(x))^{2}+x\cdot 2y(x)y'(x)=1 \end{equation*}
\begin{equation*} x^{2}y'+2xyy'=1-2xy-y^{2} \end{equation*}
\begin{equation*} (x^{2}+2xy)y'=1-2xy-y^{2} \end{equation*}
\begin{equation*} y'=\dfrac{1-2xy-y^2}{x^2+2xy} \end{equation*}
