Exercise 4.

Write the functions as a composition of at least three different functions. Note that there are several ways to do this, so the answer is not unique.
  1. \(\displaystyle f(x)=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{x^{2}+1}}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. \(\displaystyle f(x)=3(x-1)^{3/2}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. \(\displaystyle f(x)=\dfrac{\sqrt{(1+2x)^{3}}+2\sqrt{1+2x}-1-2x}{\sqrt{1+2x}}\)
    🔗
    🔗
🔗
Solution.
  1. \(f=f_{1}\circ f_{2} \circ f_{3}\text{,}\) where \(f_{1}(x)=\dfrac{1}{x},f_{2}(x)=\sqrt{x},f_{3}(x)=x^{2}+1\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. \(f=f_{1}\circ f_{2} \circ f_{3}\text{,}\) where \(f_{1}(x)=3x,f_{2}(x)=x^{3/2},f_{3}(x)=x-1\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. \(f=f_{1}\circ f_{2} \circ f_{3}\text{,}\) where \(f_{1}(x)=1+2x,f_{2}(x)=\sqrt{x},f_{3}(x)=\dfrac{x^3+2x-x^2}{x}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
🔗
