Exercise 4.

Determine the degree, leading coefficient, leading term and constant term of the following polynomials.
  1. \(\displaystyle -(1-x)^{2}+2(x-1)(2x+1)-(x-2)(x+2)\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. \(\displaystyle (x-2)(x^{2}+2x+4)-(x-2)^{3}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. \(\displaystyle (x^{2}+x-6)^{2}-2x(x+3)(x^{2}+1)\)
    🔗
    🔗
🔗
Solution.
  1. \begin{equation*} &-(1-x)^{2}+2(x-1)(2x+1)-(x-2)(x+2)\\&=-(1-2x+x^{2})+2(2x^{2}+x-2x-1)-(x^{2}-4)\\&=-1+2x-x^{2}+4x^{2}+2x-4x-2-x^{2}+4\\&=2x^{2}+1 \end{equation*}
    The degree of the polynomial is 2, the leading coefficient is \(2\text{,}\) the leading term is \(2x^{2}\) and the constant term is \(1\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. \begin{equation*} &(x-2)(x^{2}+2x+4)-(x-2)^{3}\\&=(x^{3}-2^{3})-(x^{3}-6x^{2}+12x-8)\\&=x^{3}-8-x^{3}+6x^{2}-12x+8\\&=6x^{2}-12x \end{equation*}
    The degree of the polynomial is 2, the leading coefficient is 6, the leading term is \(6x^{2}\) and the constant term is \(0\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. \begin{equation*} &(x^{2}+x-6)^{2}-2x(x+3)(x^{2}+1)\\&=(x^{4}+x^{2}+6^{2}+2x^{3}-12x^{2}-12x)-2x(x^{3}+x+3x^{2}+3)\\&=x^{4}+2x^{3}-11x^{2}-12x+36-2x^{4}-2x^{2}-6x^{3}-6x\\&=-x^{4}-4x^{3}-13x^{2}-18x+36 \end{equation*}
    The degree of the polynomial is 4, the leading coefficient is -1, the leading term is \(-x^{4}\) and the constant term is 36.
