Exercise 3.

Let \(n\) and \(x\) be positive integers. Write the following expression \(\dfrac{\sqrt[3]{x^{6n}x^{-3n-9}}}{\sqrt{x^{2(n+2)}}}\) in the form \(x^{k}\text{,}\) for some integer \(k\text{.}\)
🔗
Solution.
\(\dfrac{\sqrt[3]{x^{6n}x^{-3n-9}}}{\sqrt{x^{2(n+2)}}}=\dfrac{x^{6n/3}x^{(-3n-9)/3}}{x^{2(n+2)/2}}=\dfrac{x^{2n}x^{-n-3}}{x^{n+2}}=\dfrac{x^{2n-n-3}}{x^{n+2}}=\dfrac{x^{n-3}}{x^{n+2}}=x^{(n-3)-(n+2)}=x^{-5}.\)
