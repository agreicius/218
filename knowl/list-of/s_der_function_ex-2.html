Exercise 2.

Using the limit definition of the derivative, find the equation of the tangent line to the function \(f(x)=(1+x)^{2}\) at the point \((1,4)\text{.}\) Write your solution in the form \(y=mx+b\text{.}\)
🔗
Solution.
\begin{equation*} m=f'(1)&=\displaystyle{\lim_{h \to 0} \dfrac{f(1+h)-f(1)}{h}}\\&=\displaystyle{\lim_{h \to 0}\dfrac{(1+1+h)^2-(1+1)^2}{h}}\\&=\displaystyle{\lim_{h\to 0}\dfrac{(2+h)^2-4}{h}}\\&=\displaystyle{\lim_{h\to 0}\dfrac{4+4h+h^2-4}{h}}\\&=\displaystyle{\lim_{h\to 0}\dfrac{4h+h^2}{h}}\\&=\displaystyle{\lim_{h \to 0}\dfrac{h(4+h)}{h}}\\&=\displaystyle{\lim_{h\to 0}(4+h)}\\&=4 \end{equation*}
Therefore, the equation of the tangent line is \(y-4=4(x-1)\) or \(y=4x\text{.}\)
