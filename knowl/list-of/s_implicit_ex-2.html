Exercise 2.

Assuming \(x=x(y)\) is a differentiable function, find \(\dfrac{dx}{dy}\) if \(x^{2}y+xy^{2}=x-1\text{.}\)
🔗
Solution.
\begin{equation*} (x(y))^{2}y+x(y)y^{2}=x(y)-1 \end{equation*}
\begin{equation*} \dfrac{d}{dy}[(x(y))^{2}y+x(y)y^{2}]=\dfrac{d}{dy}[x(y)-1] \end{equation*}
\begin{equation*} 2x(y)x'(y)y+x^{2}+x'(y)y^{2}+2xy=x'(y) \end{equation*}
\begin{equation*} 2xyx'+y^{2}x'-x'=-x^{2}-2xy \end{equation*}
\begin{equation*} (2xy+y^{2}-1)x'=-x^{2}-2xy \end{equation*}
\begin{equation*} x'=\dfrac{-x^2-2xy}{2xy+y^2-1} \end{equation*}
